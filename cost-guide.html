What does composite
cladding & fence cost?

Straight, current pricing for wood-plastic composite cladding and fence in the LA market — and how buying stocked material from our Chatsworth showroom compares to a fully installed job.

Last updated: June 2026

WPC (wood-plastic composite) gives you the warm look of wood without the rot, fading, or yearly upkeep. The catch most homeowners hit is price — a fully installed composite project in Los Angeles can run well into five figures. The biggest lever is simple: buy the material from a local stocking supplier and skip the distributor markup and long lead times. Here's what that looks like in real numbers.

Cladding cost

Our HLC-49 boards are 8-5/8″ wide and cover about 6 sq ft per 108″ board after the standard overlap. That puts WalPanel material at roughly $5.90 / sq ft — well under typical installed composite cladding.

Fence cost

Our fence kits are $248 per 6 ft × 6 ft bay (aluminum top and hardware included) — about $41 / linear foot in material. An installed composite fence in Los Angeles typically runs $90–$130+ / linear foot, and Trex material alone often starts around $60+ / ft.

What drives the price

Four things move the number most: quantity (volume pricing kicks in on larger cladding orders), length & color (108″ vs 114″ boards, stocked vs special-order colors), delivery (local delivery is quoted per job within ~60 miles, or pick up same-day in Chatsworth), and installation (DIY, your own contractor, or one of our WalPanel Pros). Because we stock locally and sell factory-direct, the material line is where you save the most.

How to get your number

Use our instant estimator for a material ballpark in about a minute, then send it over for a firm written quote — usually within one business day. Prices here are current for 2026 and exclude 9.75% CA sales tax; delivery is quoted separately.
