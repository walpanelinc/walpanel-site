Glendale FAQs

Good to know.

Do you deliver to Glendale?

Yes — Glendale is inside our local delivery zone, quoted per job from the Chatsworth warehouse. Many Glendale customers visit once to approve a full-length sample, then have the order delivered.

How far is the showroom?

About 25 miles — roughly 30–40 minutes via the 118 or 101/134. Open by appointment any day of the week; you can take a sample home. Book a visit →

How much does a WPC privacy fence cost here?

Fence kits are $248 per 6 ft × 6 ft set — about $41 per linear foot in material — versus $90–$130+ per linear foot for an installed composite fence in the LA area. See the full cost guide.

Is WPC a good fit for hillside homes?

Yes — it won't rot, rust, or feed termites, and the UV shell keeps color on exposed slopes. Modular 6-ft bays make stepped or terraced runs practical, one section at a time.
