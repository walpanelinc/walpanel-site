Do you sell WPC fence in Chatsworth?

Yes — our warehouse and showroom are in Chatsworth at 21350 Lassen St, Unit E17. We stock black and brown WPC fence kits at $248 per 6 ft × 6 ft set, ready for same-day or next-day pickup by appointment.

Fence kits are $248 per 6 ft × 6 ft set — about $41 per linear foot in material — versus $90–$130+ per linear foot for an installed composite fence in the LA area. See the full cost guide.
