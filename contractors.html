Trade program

Built for the
people who build.

If you're a licensed contractor, GC, or landscape architect, we want to work with you. Tell us about your business and we'll set you up with tiered pricing, dedicated quote turnaround, and a referral bonus for every customer you send our way.

What you get

The WalPanel
Pros program.

Three tiers, built to reward the contractors and builders who keep us in their rotation. You start earning trade pricing on day one and unlock cash back and visibility as you grow with us.

01

Trade pricing

Tiered discount below retail, layered with project-specific quotes. Lock in your tier with one approved application — it applies across every order.

02

Same-day quotes

Send us your project scope and a sales lead turns it into a written quote — usually same day during business hours.

03

Referral bonus

Send us a new customer whose order exceeds $500 and earn a $50 bonus. Stackable — refer as many as you like.

04

Quarterly cash back

Pro-tier members and above earn 2–3% cash back on all purchases, paid quarterly by Zelle or Visa gift card.
