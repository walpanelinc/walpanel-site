Trade program

Built for the
people who build.

If you're a licensed contractor, GC, or landscape architect, we want to work with you. Submit your license and we'll set you up with tiered pricing, dedicated quote turnaround, and a referral bonus for every customer you send our way.

What you get

Three tier program.

Pricing follows industry standards plus our standing volume discount program. Once you're approved, your pricing applies across every order — no need to negotiate per job.

01

Trade pricing

Tiered discount below retail, layered with project-specific quotes. Lock in your tier with one approved application.

02

Same-day quotes

Send us your project scope and a sales lead turns it into a written quote — usually same day during business hours.

03

Referral bonus

Refer a customer who places an order and earn credit toward your next purchase. Stackable across multiple referrals.

04

Priority pickup

Contractor orders get scheduled ahead of walk-ins. Same-day or next-day pickup with a phone call.
