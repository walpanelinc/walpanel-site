Accessibility statement

Last updated · June 2026

WalPanel Inc. is committed to making walpanelinc.com usable for as many people as possible, including people with disabilities. We want everyone to be able to learn about our products, get a quote, and reach our team.

Our goal

We aim to bring this website into substantial conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, hearing, cognitive, and motor impairments. Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and we continue to review and improve the site over time.

Measures we take

  • Descriptive alternative text for images so screen readers can convey them;
  • Semantic page structure and a declared page language;
  • Keyboard-operable navigation and a layout that reflows and supports zoom on phones and small screens;
  • Labels on form fields so they can be identified by assistive technology;
  • Periodic review of the site as we add or update content.

Ongoing work & limitations

Despite our efforts, some content may not yet be fully accessible. We are continually working to improve, and we treat accessibility barriers as issues to be fixed. If you encounter a part of the site that is difficult to use with assistive technology, please let us know (see below) and we will work to address it.

Need help, or can't access something?

If you have difficulty using any part of this website, or you'd like information or to place an order in a different way, we're glad to help directly:

  • Call or text: (858) 256-6236
  • Email: walpanelsales@gmail.com

We will do our best to provide the information, product, or service you need through one of these channels.

Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of this site. Please tell us about any barriers you run into and any suggestions for improvement using the contact details above. When you report an issue, we aim to respond within a few business days and to work with you toward a resolution.
