Project quote

Send your project scope and we'll come back with a written quote — usually within one business day.

Delivery quote

Local delivery is $150–$500 within ~60 miles, quoted per job. Give us the details below and a delivery partner will quote within 1 business day.

Sample order

Samples are $15 refundable deposit for showroom pickup, or $20 non-refundable for mail/ship. Tell us which colors and we'll have them ready.

Installation quote

We work with a trusted installation partner, or you're welcome to use your own contractor — you can also browse our WalPanel Pros. Tell us about your project and we'll connect you with the right installer. We recommend visiting the showroom first so you can see and approve the product hands-on.

Custom color order

For colors we don't stock, we can source through drop ship or special order. There's no special minimum beyond our standard order minimum (15 boards / 5 fence kits) for pickup or delivery — direct drop ship from the factory starts at 100 boards or 50 fence sets. Terms depend on the color, quantity, and timeline; tell us what you need and we'll come back with specifics. Custom orders are typically pre-paid via Zelle.
