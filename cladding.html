Good to know

Cladding FAQs

What is WPC cladding?

WPC (wood-plastic composite) cladding is a dual-layer exterior board with a UV-resistant outer shell bonded to a structural core. It gives the warm look of wood without the rot, fading, or yearly upkeep, which makes it well suited to Southern California exteriors.

What sizes and colors do WalPanel cladding boards come in?

Our HLC-49 boards are 8-5/8″ wide and come in 108″ and 114″ lengths, covering about 6 sq ft per 108″ board after the overlap. Stocked colors are Teak, Teak Wood Grain, Dark Teak, Dark Teak/Black, SPG/Black, and Black; other colors are available by special order.

How much does WPC cladding cost in Los Angeles?

WalPanel cladding starts at $35.36 per 108″ board — roughly $5.90 per square foot in material — versus $8 to $20 per square foot for installed composite cladding. See our Los Angeles cost guide for a full breakdown.

Is there a minimum order for cladding?

Yes, the minimum cladding order is 15 boards. Larger orders qualify for volume pricing, so request a written quote and we'll price it for your project.

Can I pick up the same day, and do you deliver in Los Angeles?

In-stock cladding is ready for same-day or next-day pickup by appointment at our Chatsworth showroom. Local delivery is quoted per job within about 60 miles, covering Los Angeles, Santa Monica, the San Fernando Valley, and surrounding areas.

How does WPC cladding compare to Trex and wood?

WPC resists rot, insects, and fading better than natural wood and is stocked factory-direct, so material costs run well below typical Trex retail. See our WPC vs Trex vs wood comparison for the details.
