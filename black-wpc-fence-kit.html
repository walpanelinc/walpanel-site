Good to know

Black fence FAQs

How much does the black WPC fence kit cost?

$248 per 6 ft × 6 ft set — about $41 per linear foot in material. Minimum order is 5 sets; 9.75% CA sales tax applies. Get a written quote with your run length.

Do I need a new kit for each section?

No — each bay after the first only needs one extra post at $90, so a long run is far cheaper than a kit per section.

Can I pick up the same day?

Yes. Black kits are stocked at our Chatsworth showroom for same-day or next-day pickup by appointment, with local delivery within about 60 miles. See WPC fence in Chatsworth.

What other colors are available?

Fence kits are also stocked in brown. See the full fence page for both colors, the calculator, and matching gates.
