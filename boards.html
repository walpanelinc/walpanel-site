HLC-49 Series · Outdoor cladding

Cladding boards.
A palette, two lengths.

Dual-layer WPC with a UV-resistant outer shell bonded to a structural core. 8-5/8" wide, with about 8" of coverage per board after overlap.

Outdoor only · Dual-layer WPC

Engineered for
weather and time.

Wood-Plastic Composite resists rot, insects, and UV fade — the manufactured outer shell adds an extra layer of color stability that single-layer composites don't have. Direct factory pricing, in stock for pickup. Minimum order is 15 pcs.
