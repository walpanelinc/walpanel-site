Installation

Six steps.
One afternoon.

The kit is designed so installers can stand up a bay solo in about an hour. Iron base feet bolt to a flat solid surface (typically a concrete footing); aluminum post and cover slot in, panels slide in horizontally, top cap finishes the run.

Full installation guide (PDF)

Step-by-step illustrated guide with measurements, sequence, and hardware reference. Bring this to the job site.

Download PDF
01

Set the base

Bolt iron base feet to concrete or solid ground. Measure spacing per bay (6 ft on center) and use the hardware included in the kit.

02

Mount the posts

Aluminum posts and post covers slot into the iron feet in sequence. Use the corner brackets at any direction change.

03

Slide the panels

9 panels slide horizontally into the post channels. Bottom cover seats first, panels stack up, top cover finishes.

04

Cap & done

Aluminum caps press onto each post for a clean finished line. The included hardware locks panels and post covers in place.
