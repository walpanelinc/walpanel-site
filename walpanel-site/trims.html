Where they go

Three common uses.

L-Corners cover the exposed end-grain at any 90° corner. They lock in alignment, hide cut edges, and pull the whole installation together visually.

01

Outside corners

Wrap the exterior of any 90° change in direction. The L sits over the board ends and hides the substrate.

02

Inside corners

Use the L as a finished transition where two walls meet inward — cleaner than mitered board cuts.

03

End caps

At wall terminations (around windows, doorways, openings), the L gives a finished edge that won't show wear over time.

04

Match the boards

Match your trim color to the dominant board color, or accent with a contrasting color for emphasis. Most jobs use Black for an industrial feel.
