Woodland Hills FAQs

Good to know.

Where can I buy WPC fence near Woodland Hills?

Our warehouse and showroom are at 21350 Lassen St, Unit E17 in Chatsworth — about 20 minutes up Topanga Canyon Blvd. We stock black and brown WPC fence kits at $248 per 6 ft × 6 ft set, ready for same-day or next-day pickup by appointment.

Will WPC hold up in Woodland Hills heat?

Yes — that's what it's engineered for. Woodland Hills is one of the hottest parts of LA, and our dual-layer boards use a UV-resistant shell over a structural core, so they resist fading, warping, and cracking in extreme sun. Compare materials in our WPC vs Trex guide.

How much does a WPC privacy fence cost here?

Fence kits are $248 per 6 ft × 6 ft set — about $41 per linear foot in material — versus $90–$130+ per linear foot for an installed composite fence in the LA area. See the full cost guide.

Do you deliver to Woodland Hills, Tarzana, and Calabasas?

Yes — delivery is quoted per job across Woodland Hills, West Hills, Canoga Park, Tarzana, Calabasas, Encino, and the wider Valley. Or pick up same-day at the showroom.
