Burbank FAQs

Good to know.

Do you deliver to Burbank?

Yes — Burbank is inside our local delivery zone, quoted per job from the Chatsworth warehouse (about 25 minutes away). Or pick up same-day by appointment.

Is WPC fence good for small lots and ADUs?

Very. The 6 ft × 6 ft modular bays build clean privacy lines along tight side yards and ADU setbacks, and panels finish clean on both faces — your neighbor's side looks as good as yours.

How much does a WPC privacy fence cost here?

Fence kits are $248 per 6 ft × 6 ft set — about $41 per linear foot in material — versus $90–$130+ per linear foot for an installed composite fence in the LA area. See the full cost guide.

Can I see the colors before ordering?

Yes — visit the Chatsworth showroom by appointment to see full-length samples of every stocked color and take one home ($15 refundable at pickup, $20 mailed). Book a visit →
